# Standing deer logo iterations

These are exploratory Doe logo candidates only. They do not replace the active
logo at `assets/doe-logo.svg`.

The direction is a grown adult deer in the same polygonal purple/red/blue
family as `assets/logos/doe/2026-06-23-doe-logo-release-evidence.svg`, but
standing upright with a proud posture instead of a low/resting pose.

Files:

- `standing-deer-proud-v1.svg` - antlerless adult doe, broad side profile.
- `standing-deer-proud-v2.svg` - taller antlerless adult doe, longer legs.
- `standing-deer-proud-v3-stag.svg` - mature stag-like variant with antlers.
- `standing-deer-proud-v4-badge.svg` - compact badge-like standing silhouette.
